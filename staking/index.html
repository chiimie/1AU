How staking works

  1. Stake any amountYour 1AU move into the staking part of the contract. They are never burned: storms only burn from the pool, never from stakers or wallets.
  2. Wait one full dayNew stakes start earning at the second daily update after you join, so nobody can jump in a minute before 10:30 UTC and grab a day they did not sail.
  3. Earn on calm daysBelow 600 km/s the coin gives back part of what storms burned: the calmer the wind, the bigger the part (1.17% of what is owed at 426 km/s, 1.39% at 300). 30% of it is split among all earning stakers, by stake size.
  4. Storms come firstAbove 600 km/s nothing is given back that day: coins are burned from the pool, which pushes the price up, and what burns is what the next calm days return. Before the first storm nothing is owed, so there are no rewards yet.
  5. Claim when you likeRewards pile up in the contract. Claim them at any time: they land in your wallet, where they also receive the holders' 10%.
  6. Leave at any time"Ask to leave" stops your coins from earning at once. They unlock at the first daily update that comes at least 24 hours after your last stake (the minimum is one day); then press Withdraw.
Nothing is created from nothing. Everything paid to stakers comes from coins that storms burned earlier, so the supply never grows past where it started. If nobody is staking, the stakers' part simply waits.
