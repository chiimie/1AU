How staking works

  1. Stake any amountYour 1AU move into the staking part of the contract. They are never burned: storms only burn from the pool, never from stakers or wallets.
  2. Wait one full dayNew stakes start earning at the second daily update after you join, so nobody can jump in a minute before 10:30 UTC and grab a day they did not sail.
  3. Earn on every calm dayWhen the wind is at or below 600 km/s, the coin mints 0.035% of the supply × (wind ÷ 426)² and 30% of it is split among all earning stakers, by stake size. Faster wind, bigger day.
  4. Storm days pay nothingAbove 600 km/s there is no mint, so no staking rewards that day. Instead, coins are burned from the pool, which pushes the price up.
  5. Claim when you likeRewards pile up in the contract. Claim them at any time: they land in your wallet, where they also receive the holders' 10%.
  6. Leave at any time"Ask to leave" stops your coins from earning at once. They unlock at the first daily update that comes at least 24 hours after your last stake (the minimum is one day); then press Withdraw.
The brake favours stakers. If the supply grows more than 10% over 365 days (only possible with a very quiet Sun), holders stop receiving first. Stakers keep earning, on a share that fades to zero at 15%.
