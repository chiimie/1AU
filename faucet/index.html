How the faucet works

  1. Connect a wallet on Base SepoliaMetaMask, Coinbase Wallet or any browser wallet. The page switches the network for you.
  2. Keep a little test ETHThe wallet needs at least 0.0005 Base Sepolia ETH. It pays the gas and keeps bots from emptying the faucet with thousands of empty wallets.
  3. Claim once a dayThe faucet opens again after every daily update at 10:30 UTC. Up to 800 claims per day, then it closes until the next update.
  4. The wind sets the amount50,000 × wind ÷ 426. A calm 300 km/s day gives about 35,000 1AU; a 600 km/s day about 70,000; a strong storm at 850 km/s almost 100,000.

The faucet holds 20% of the supply (29.9 billion 1AU) and still has —. It works only from a regular wallet, not from a smart-contract wallet.
