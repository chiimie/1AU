The rules

Storms burn. Calm days give back.

No team decides anything. The Sun does, once a day. 600 km/s is the line: the further the wind goes from it, the more happens, but each extra 100 km/s counts less than the one before. Wallet balances never go down.

☼

Below 600 km/s: give back

Calm days return what storms burned. The calmer the wind, the bigger the share that day: 0.6% of what is owed at 550 km/s, 1.17% at 426, 1.39% at 300. On average a storm comes back within about 90 days.

Above 600 km/s: storm

What burns comes back

The coin keeps one number from the day it was published: coins burned minus coins given back. Calm days only return part of it, never more, so the supply can never grow past the 149,597,870,700 it started with. Until the first storm there is nothing to give back.

Where the wind comes from

Measured in space, signed on Earth.

NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center publishes real-time solar wind data from spacecraft parked at L1, between the Earth and the Sun. Four independent Opsikom operators, on three different servers, read it and sign the same 24-hour mean. The contract accepts it only with 3 of 4 signatures and publishes the median.

    Honest small print.

    Is this worth money?

    No. 1AU lives on Base Sepolia, a test network. Test ETH and 1AU have no value. The contract is a draft and has not been audited.

    Can storms be predicted?

    Partly. The Sun rotates about every 27 days, so some fast wind streams come back, and space-weather forecasters announce storms a few days ahead. The coin cannot be front-run on the day itself, because the wind is published and applied in the same transaction.

    What if the data stops?

    If no new reading arrives, nothing happens: no mint, no burn. A reading older than 2 days is ignored. Anyone can run the daily update, so the coin does not depend on one server.

    Single source?

    Yes: the solar wind comes from NOAA SWPC, one agency. The four operators make sure nobody can change the number on its way to the chain, not that the source is right.

    Why is nothing minted yet?

    Calm days only give back what storms burned, counted from the day the coin was published. Until the first storm above 600 km/s nothing is owed, so nothing is minted.

    Will my balance ever shrink?

    No. Storms burn only from the V2 pool. Holders and stakers can only receive.
